Creative Commons Attribution-NoDerivatives 4.0 International (CC-BY-ND-4.0),
separately from the application's AGPL-3.0-only license. The artwork is visually unchanged.
An accessibility title was added to the SVG; PNGs are resized format reproductions.

Regenerate the PNGs and their cache versions from `frontend/` with
`bun scripts/branding-assets.mjs` (requires `rsvg-convert` and ImageMagick).
This also embeds the unchanged artwork in the backend's stock subscription-page config.
